# Dead Mansion — Hero v1.0 (APPROVED)

Status: approved by project owner on 2026-10-20. Do not redesign without approval.

## Visual identity
- Original male zombie hunter, side-view pixel-art action-platformer protagonist.
- Art direction: between Dead Cells (expressive detail) and Broforce (compact action silhouette), approximately 60/40 as a working visual guideline.
- Dark burgundy/red baseball cap.
- Short facial stubble; NOT a moustache.
- Black leather jacket.
- Blue jeans, slightly longer than earlier concept, covering the tops of boots.
- Boots, gloves, double-barrel shotgun; a backpack appears in approved concept.
- Readable silhouette at gameplay scale; coherent proportions across all poses.
- Working frame size proposal: ~48x64 pixels, to validate against game scale, not an irrevocable constraint.

## Animation requirements
- Idle/ready stance.
- Heavy breathing: visibly moving shoulders/chest, after sustained running or shooting.
- Running: energetic leg and arm motion.
- Jumping: takeoff and airborne frames.
- Landing: clear impact, bent knees and recovery; optional dust on hard landing.
- Shooting: recoil, muzzle flash, weapon motion.
- Taking damage: obvious recoil/knockback, loss of balance and recovery, not just a color flash.
- Death: legible multi-frame fall.

## Production and acceptance
- First deliver production-usable transparent sprites for idle, heavy breathing and run; approve their consistency in-game before producing the full set.
- All frames must have consistent scale, anchor/pivot and ground baseline, and no accidental background baked in.
- Separate character animation frames from effects (muzzle flashes, dust, blood) where practical.
- Concept illustration is NOT itself a usable sprite sheet. Do not claim it is.
- Do not use ripped or copyrighted third-party sprites.
- Maintain existing controls and gameplay throughout integration.
